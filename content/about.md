# Why this site exists

I am Yoonsoo Park, a software engineer who learns by building, operating, and writing down what survives contact with real systems.

This site is public evidence of that growth. I turn experiments, production lessons, corrected assumptions, and architectural decisions into material another engineer can use at work. When an older approach stops being sound, I keep its history visible and point to the maintained replacement.

## What you will find

- AWS and agent systems with explicit security, cost, and operational boundaries.
- Software architecture and platform decisions with their tradeoffs intact.
- Salesforce, Python, TypeScript, DevOps, and self-hosting field notes.
- Article Atlas expeditions that connect several sources into one practical decision.

Start with the [safe agent operations expedition](/expeditions/safe-agent-operations.html), [browse every article](/articles.html), or [explore Article Atlas](/explore/).

The name “thats.nono” comes from documenting the pitfalls—the moments where a shortcut looks reasonable but fails under real constraints—so the next person does not need to repeat them.

You can inspect the work on [GitHub](https://github.com/ypark9/yopa.page) or [contact me](mailto:yoonsoo@duck.com).
